About SNGU Dhule

A Legacy of Excellence Under Shri Vile Parle Kelavani Mandal (SVKM)

SVKM NMIMS Global University (SNGU) at Dhule represents a landmark in premier higher education across North Maharashtra. Built upon SVKM’s distinguished 90-year legacy of managing premier institutions across Mumbai, Shirpur, Hyderabad, and Bengaluru, SNGU Dhule brings global-standard academic pedagogy and research infrastructure to the region.
